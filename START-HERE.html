Read your lectures properly

Slides and transcripts become lessons you can highlight, annotate and revise from, in your browser, on your own machine. Three steps, and no terminal.

Already have lessons in this folder? Then you need none of the steps below: double-click Start Study Hub.command, here in this folder, and your browser opens on the reader. The first time only, macOS asks you to right-click it and choose Open, because it came from the internet. You do not need to know what Python is; if your Mac asks to install something, it is Apple's own one-off installer and the script explains it.

  1. Install Claude

    Get the desktop app from claude.ai/download and sign in. If you already have it, skip this.

  2. Open this folder in it

    Start a Code session and use the folder picker to choose the folder this page is sitting in.

  3. Say what you want

    Type set me up and press return. It writes its settings, starts Study Hub, opens it in your browser, and asks what you would like to read.

What happens after that

It will ask where your lessons are coming from. You can import lessons somebody sent you, download a course from KEATS in your own browser, or point it at a folder of slides and transcripts you already have.

It will also offer to keep Study Hub running after you restart your machine. Say yes, bookmark the page it opens, and you are finished with the setup for good.

Everything stays on your computer. Your highlights and notes are ordinary files on your own disk, beside your lessons. There is no account, nothing is uploaded, and there is nothing to pay for.

Prefer to do it by hand? README.md in this folder has the two commands, and the instructions in .claude/skills/ read perfectly well as documentation.
